✨ What is Karaoke Night?Features and how a night works

Search any song, add it to the queue and sing while the lyrics follow the music. Your microphone scores you as you sing. Play solo on one device, or create a room so friends anywhere can join with a code: a shared queue, live voice, ready checks, duets and tournaments, reactions and chat.

🎶Synced lyrics
Lyrics follow the music, with easy tools to fix the timing.
⭐Live scoring
Your mic scores your tone, volume and timing as you sing.
🌐Sing together
Live voice, shared queue, duets and tournaments with friends.
🔥Crowd energy
Reactions, stars and chat from everyone in the room.
  1. Pick a name
  2. Sing solo or open a room
  3. Queue songs and sing

Karaoke Night is a free project made for fun, not a commercial service. It is a karaoke / sing-along style app: songs play through YouTube’s official embedded player (nothing is downloaded or stored here), synced lyrics come from LRCLIB, and karaoke versions are other people’s YouTube uploads. All songs, lyrics and videos belong to their owners. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube, YouTube Music or LRCLIB, and availability, lyric timing and karaoke quality are not guaranteed.
