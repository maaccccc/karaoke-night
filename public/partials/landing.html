Karaoke Night

Sing your favourite songs with time-synced lyrics, live scoring and your friends, whether they are next to you or across the world.

Search any song on the search box, add it to the queue and sing while the lyrics follow the music. Your microphone scores you as you sing. Play solo on one device, or create a room so friends anywhere can join with a code: a shared queue, live voice, ready checks, duets and tournaments, reactions and chat.

Karaoke Night is a free project made for fun, not a commercial service. It is a karaoke / sing-along style app: songs play through YouTube’s official embedded player (nothing is downloaded or stored here), synced lyrics come from LRCLIB, and karaoke versions are other people’s YouTube uploads. All songs, lyrics and videos belong to their owners. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube, YouTube Music or LRCLIB, and availability, lyric timing and karaoke quality are not guaranteed.
