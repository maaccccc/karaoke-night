❓ How Karaoke Night works

Tap a topic to open it.

🎤 Quick start (one device)
  1. Add the singers’ names under Singers.
  2. Search a song, pick who sings it, and press Add to queue. Look for synced lyrics ✓; songs without timed lyrics are skipped. Press ▶ Preview first if you are not sure it is the right song.
  3. Press Start the next song. The lyrics follow the music and your mic scores you as you sing.

On one shared device people take turns: whoever sang longest ago goes next.

🎶 Karaoke versions (no original singer)

With Karaoke versions ticked under the search box, adding a song looks for a karaoke / instrumental video of it, so you hear the backing track instead of the original singer. The queue shows “🎤 karaoke version” when one was found. If none exists, the original plays (that is the sing-along style).

The video box shows a still cover while the karaoke track plays. You can switch that off in Settings.

⏱ Lyrics out of sync?
  • Sooner / Later buttons nudge the lyrics a little.
  • Mark: click a lyric line, press Mark picked line the moment you hear it start, then mark another line near the end. The app fixes the shift and stretch, and remembers it for that video in your browser.
  • Audio delay in Settings helps with Bluetooth speakers. Reset timing starts over.
  • You can also pick another lyrics version from the list on the singing page.
🌐 Sing online with friends (rooms)
  • On the home page choose Create a room (private = only people with the code, public = listed in the lobby), or Join with a code.
  • Press 🌐 Room to copy an invite link or show a QR code.
  • Everyone shares one queue. Before each song the singer picks Solo, Duet or Tournament, then everyone presses I’m ready! and a 3-2-1 countdown starts the song for all at once.
  • Wear headphones when you sing, or friends will hear the song twice.
  • The host can skip for everyone; others can vote to skip.
⭐ Scoring and fun
  • Up to 50 points from your mic (tone, volume and timing), a combo bonus of up to 10 for singing lines well in a row, and up to 10 bonus points from the audience’s 1 to 5 stars.
  • Duets split the lines between two singers. In a tournament, whoever scores most on their own lines wins a 10-point bonus.
  • Listeners can send reactions, chat, and earn funny awards at the end of each song. End the night shows the winner and highlights.
🎧 Microphone tips
  • Use 🎤 Mic test before the first song to check that you are heard.
  • If the meter barely moves, raise Mic sensitivity in Settings.
  • TV, fans or family in the room? Set Mic suppression to Strong or Maximum under Sound in Settings. It learns the background and quiets your mic until you are clearly louder.
  • Mic too sensitive? Set Mic threshold in Settings (like Discord’s input sensitivity): your mic is muted below the white line.
  • In voice chat, right-click a person (press and hold on a phone) to change how loud they are for you. Voice chat volume (everyone) goes up to 200%.
  • The browser must be allowed to use your microphone. Chrome, Edge and Brave work best; keep the tab visible while singing.
🎨 Looks and settings

Pick a theme on the welcome page. Use ⚙ Settings, grouped into Sound, Sync & timing and Preferences: microphone and suppression, audio delay, lyrics size, reduce motion, smooth mode for slow phones, the still karaoke cover and more.

ℹ️ About this project

Karaoke Night is a free project made for fun, not a commercial service. It is a karaoke / sing-along style app: songs play through YouTube’s official embedded player (nothing is downloaded or stored here), synced lyrics come from LRCLIB, and karaoke versions are other people’s YouTube uploads. All songs, lyrics and videos belong to their owners. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube, YouTube Music or LRCLIB, and availability, lyric timing and karaoke quality are not guaranteed.
